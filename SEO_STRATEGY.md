# Floyd Equipment Rental SEO Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines the SEO standards for the Floyd Equipment Rental website.

Our goal is to help people in and around Floyd County find the equipment they need while keeping every page helpful, natural, and easy to read.

---

# SEO Philosophy

People first.

Search engines second.

Never sacrifice readability to add keywords.

Every page should answer a real customer question.

---

# Target Market

Primary Service Area:
- Floyd County, Virginia

Secondary Service Areas:
- Nearby communities that are realistically served by Floyd Equipment Rental.

Do not claim service areas the business does not actually serve.

---

# Page Structure

Every page should have:

- One H1 heading
- Clear H2 sections
- Logical H3 headings when needed

Do not skip heading levels.

---

# URL Naming

Use short, descriptive URLs.

Examples:

- index.html
- commercial-brush-mower.html
- reserve.html
- about.html
- contact.html

Use lowercase letters and hyphens only.

---

# Page Titles

Recommended format:

Equipment Name | Floyd Equipment Rental

Examples:

Commercial Brush Mower | Floyd Equipment Rental

About Floyd Equipment Rental

Reserve Equipment | Floyd Equipment Rental

Keep titles under approximately 60 characters when practical.

---

# Meta Descriptions

Target 140–160 characters.

Describe the page naturally.

Include:
- Equipment name (when applicable)
- Floyd Equipment Rental
- A customer benefit

---

# Equipment Pages

Each equipment page should naturally include:

- Equipment name
- Common uses
- Ideal projects
- Rental availability
- Trailer included (when applicable)
- Delivery available (when applicable)

Avoid repeating keywords unnaturally.

---

# Images

Use descriptive filenames.

Good:
commercial-brush-mower.jpg

Avoid:
IMG_1234.jpg

Every image should include descriptive alt text.

---

# Internal Linking

Homepage links to:
- Every equipment page
- About
- Contact
- Reserve

Every equipment page links back to:
- Homepage
- Reserve
- Contact

---

# Local SEO

Mention Floyd County naturally where appropriate.

Create and maintain:
- Google Business Profile
- Consistent business name, address, and phone number (NAP)
- Service area information

---

# Performance

Optimize image sizes.

Use modern image formats when practical.

Keep pages fast-loading.

---

# Guiding Principle

If an SEO change makes the page less helpful to a customer, don't do it.

Helpful content wins over keyword stuffing.
