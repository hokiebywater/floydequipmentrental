Before You Rent

Is this brush mower the right fit for your project?

Best suited for

Medium to dense brush, tall grass, fence rows, trails, and overgrown fields.

Brush and vegetation

Ideal for woody weeds, saplings up to 3" diameter, and heavy ground cover.

Terrain & access

Works on firm, reasonably even ground. Avoid steep slopes and very soft mud.

Trailer requirements

Needs a standard equipment trailer and a truck with a 2" hitch for safe transport.

Important limitations

Not for large trees, thick brush over 3" diameter, or wet, boggy terrain.
