Before You Rent

Will this machine work on your property?

Best suited for

Medium brush, tall grass, fence rows, trails, and overgrown fields.

Terrain

Firm, even ground works best. Avoid steep slopes and saturated soil.

Site access

Need enough room to position and operate the mower safely.

Limitations

Not for trees over 3" diameter, heavy stumps, or boggy ground.
