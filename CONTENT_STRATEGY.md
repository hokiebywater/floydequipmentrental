# Floyd Equipment Rental Content Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines how content should be written across the Floyd Equipment Rental website.

Every page should help customers quickly understand the equipment, decide if it is right for their project, and confidently reserve it.

---

# Core Philosophy

People visit the website to answer three questions:

1. What equipment do you have?
2. Is this the right machine for my project?
3. How do I rent it?

If a section does not help answer one of those questions, simplify it or remove it.

---

# Brand Voice

Be:
- Friendly
- Professional
- Helpful
- Knowledgeable
- Local
- Straightforward

Avoid:
- Sales hype
- Corporate jargon
- Buzzwords
- Exaggerated marketing claims

Write like an experienced neighbor who knows equipment and enjoys helping people.

---

# Homepage

The homepage is the primary equipment browsing experience.

Each equipment card should include:
- Equipment photo
- Equipment name
- One- or two-sentence description
- 2–3 key highlights (for example: Trailer Included, Delivery Available)
- View Details button
- Reserve button

Do not place detailed specifications on the homepage.

---

# Equipment Detail Pages

Each equipment page should answer:

**Is this the right machine for my project?**

Recommended sections:
- Hero
- Specifications
- Perfect For
- Before You Rent
- Rental Information
- Frequently Asked Questions
- Reserve button

Include only information that helps a customer make a rental decision.

---

# Writing Style

- Keep paragraphs short.
- Prefer bullets over long paragraphs.
- Remove duplicate information.
- Use plain English.
- Explain technical terms only when necessary.
- Every sentence should provide useful information.

---

# Calls to Action

Preferred:
- View Details
- Reserve Now
- Check Availability
- Contact Us

Avoid:
- Buy Now
- Discover
- Learn More

---

# Photography

Prioritize:
- Real equipment
- Real Floyd County locations
- Real customer projects
- High-quality photography

Placeholder images are acceptable until original photos are available.

---

# SEO Content

- Write for people first.
- Use natural language.
- Include the equipment name in headings.
- Keep one primary topic per page.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every page should help visitors confidently rent equipment.

Simple is better than clever.

Helpful is better than persuasive.
